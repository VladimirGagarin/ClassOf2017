Class of 2017 biography

David Royse

Rolls Royce · Admission No. 533

David Royse, fondly known as 'Rolls Royce,' is a top-performing student celebrated for his dedication and seriousness in academics. Beyond the classroom, he's an incredible artist and rapper, using his talent to create meaningful art that resonates with many. He cherishes every moment spent at St. Christopher High, making lasting memories with his peers.
