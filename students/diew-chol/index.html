Class of 2017 biography

Diew Chol

Vendetta · Admission No. 475

Diew, known affectionately as Vendetta, was a beacon of joy and creativity whose vibrant spirit mirrored the essence of Caribbean life—full of rhythm, warmth, and passion. His legacy as a Creative Innovator will forever linger in our hearts, reminding us of his ability to bring color and imagination to everything he touched. Diew’s love for life was as vast as the ocean, and his laughter and charisma will echo in our memories like a timeless melody.
