Class of 2017 biography

Derrick Odongo

Drake · Admission No. 519

Derrick is a generous, kind, and incredibly intelligent student of our time. Known for his love of jokes and his sharp wit, he brought joy and laughter wherever he went. Dedicated and passionate about hip-hop, Derrick expressed himself with creativity and rhythm, leaving an unforgettable mark on all who knew him.
