Class of 2017 biography

Lydiah Wambui

Lydez Hialy · Admission No. 497

Lydiah Wambui, also known as Lydez Hialy, is regarded as one of the most beautiful souls to ever grace our school, often referred to as an angel for her kindness, grace, and radiant presence. Beyond her stunning beauty, she is also a top-performing student who consistently excelled academically. Lydiah's dedication to her work was unparalleled, making her a role model for her peers. A former prefect, environmentalist, and thinker, she demonstrated remarkable leadership and an unwavering commitment to making the world around her a better place. Her contributions, both inside and outside the classroom, have made a profound impact on everyone who has had the privilege of knowing her.
