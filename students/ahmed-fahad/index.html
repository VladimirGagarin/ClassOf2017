Class of 2017 biography

Ahmed Fahad

Tallest · Admission No. 507

Ahmed Fahad, fondly known as 'Tallest,' stood not only as the tallest among his peers but also as a towering figure of kindness and generosity. A devout follower of Islam, he carried his faith with grace, embodying the virtues of compassion and selflessness. Ahmed was a beacon of inspiration, always uplifting those around him with his words of encouragement and acts of benevolence. As a motivator, his wisdom and empathy resonated deeply, leaving a legacy of hope and positivity that will forever echo through the hearts of those who knew him.
